Day #3

**Partners**: Parents | **Date**: Jul 10, 2026

**Activity**: Discussed family values, education and life lessons.

**Activity Log**

After dinner, I sat with my parents on the veranda to talk about their school days and what shaped their views on education. They shared how their own parents prioritized honesty, hard work, and humility over just getting marks. Umma recalled nearly failing his first high school physics exam, expecting a scolding, but instead receiving encouragement from her father to focus on understanding the concepts instead of worrying about marks.

**Reflection**

I used to think my parents pushed me academically just to see good grades on a report card. Hearing their stories made me realize they care much more about my character and how I handle set-backs. I learned that true education isn't just about memorizing facts for an exam, it’s about building a strong moral backbone and staying grounded no matter what challenges come my way.
