Day #3

**Partner**: Mama (Uncle) | **Date**: Jul 9, 2026

**Activity**: Accompanied for a walk and discussed local history/community changes.

**Activity Log**

I wnet for an evening walk with Mama through our local neighbourhood. As we walked, he pointed out how much the whole area had changed since his childhood. New concrete houses and tarred roads replaces endless green fields, fresh streams filled with small fish and open pastures where the village cattle grazed. He shared stored of playing football in the fields and explained how the whole local community used to gather at the corner _chaya kada_ (tea shop) to discuss village news before everyone had smartphones.

**Reflection**

Walking with Mama made me see my own neighbourhood through a different lens. I realized how progress comes at the cost of open spaces and quiet traditions. hearing him talk about the close community of his youth taught me the value of staying connected with the people around us, instead of just staring at our screens inside.
