# VALUE EDUCATION PORTFOLIO (VEP) 
Engineering education is not only about acquiring technical knowledge; it is also about 
developing the values, habits, and attitudes that help us become responsible professionals, 
ethical engineers, and compassionate citizens. 
The Value Education Portfolio (VEP) is designed to encourage you to cultivate discipline, 
empathy, social responsibility, ethical awareness, personal well-being, and lifelong learning 
through meaningful experiences. 
This activity invites you to read, reflect, stay physically active, and engage positively with society. 
The objective is not merely to complete tasks but to develop habits and perspectives that 
contribute to your personal growth. 

## Component 3: Community Engagement Activity
### Option 5: Family and Community Interaction Activity 
Meaningful relationships are an important part of value-based living. 
Spend time interacting with: 
- Parents
- Grandparents
- Elderly relatives
- Elderly neighbours 
Requirement 
- At least 30 minutes per day
- Minimum 10 days 
Suggested Activities 
- Listening to life experiences and stories
- Helping with daily activities
- Assisting with digital tools and technology
- Accompanying them for walks
- Discussing values, education, and life lessons 
Evidence 
- Activity log
- Reflection diary
- Signed confirmation from a parent, guardian, or family member 
Reflection 
Describe: 
- What you learned from these interactions
- How the experience strengthened empathy, respect, and understanding
