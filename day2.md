Day #2

**Partner**: Umma | **Date**: Jul 8, 2026

**Activity**: Cooking _Payasam_ together.

**Activity Log**

This evening, I joined Umma in the kitchen to help prepare traditional _Palada Payasam_. While stirring the thick, boiling milk over the stove, I complained about how slow the process was. Umma laughed and shared how, when she was young, they didn't use ready-made mixes or gas stoves. She used to spend hours gathering firewood, grinding rice, and slowly reducing fresh cow's milk over an open flame for special occasions. She showed me how to constantly stir in a figure-eight pattern so the milk wouldn't burn at the bottom.

**Reflection**

Taking over the wooden spoon, I realized how much patience and love goes into cooking for a family. What felt like a boring chore became a lesson in mindfulness and tradition. Standing beside Umma in the warm kitchen, I learned that the best things in life can't be rushed, they take time, care, and steady effort to turn out sweet.
