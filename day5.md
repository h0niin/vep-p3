Day #5

**Partners**: Mama (Uncle) | **Date**: Jul 11, 2026

**Activity**: Helping with daily activities (gardening and harvesting).

**Activity Log**

I spent the afternoon helping my Mama, in his vegetable garden. My main tasks were clearing heavy weeds, carrying buckets of water and helping him harvest. As we worked, he taught me how to identify rich soil and explained traditional, natural pest-control methods instead of harsh chemicals. He showed me the delicate way to twist a vegetable off the vine so the plant stays healthy and keeps bearing fruit.

**Reflection**

My hands were covered in dirt and my back was tired, but seeing the pride in Mama’s eyes as we carried the full basket inside made it completely worth it. I realized how much patience it takes to nurture something from a tiny seed to dinner table food. Working alongside him taught me the value of honest labor, self-reliance, and showing respect for the land that feeds us.
