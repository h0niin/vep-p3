Day #8

**Partners**: Vallimma (Grandmother) | **Date**: Jul 14, 2026

**Activity**: Helping with household chores and discussing family history.

**Activity Log**

I spent the evening helping my Vallimma clean and organize the heavy wooden chests stored in our ancestral storeroom. While dusting old brass utensils and sorting through stacks of yellowed family photographs, my Vallimma paused to share the stories behind each item. She showed me my great-grandfather’s old pocket watch and recounted how our family used to host community feasts on the veranda during events. Helping her out with heavy lifting turned into an engaging history lesson about our lineage.

**Reflection**

Sorting through old family heritage made me realize that household chores can be a gateway to understanding where I come from. Every dusty photograph and antique object had a story of sacrifice, joy or resilience attached to it. Helping Vallimma preserve these belongings taught me that maintaining a home isn't just about keeping things clean but also about honoring the family heritage and values built within those walls over generations.
