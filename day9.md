Day #9

**Partners**: Parents | **Date**: Jul 15, 2026

**Activity**: Digital organization and memory archiving.

**Activity Log**

I spent the evening with my parents organizing decades of old family photographs stored on an old external hard drive and loose memory cards. Most files were scattered across random folders with meaningless names like "DCIM_0094." Together, we sorted the photos into year-by-year digital albums and tagged key events—like their wedding, my childhood birthdays, and family trips. While labeling the files, my parents shared the stories behind each snapshot, explaining who distant relatives were and recalling funny moments behind the camera.

**Reflection**

Sorting through thousands of digital photos made me realize that memories can easily get lost if we don't take care to preserve them. Using my digital skills to organize their digital clutter gave my parents a clean, easily accessible archive of our family history. Hearing the stories behind each picture reminded me that technology is at its best when it helps us hold onto the moments and people that matter most.
