Day #9

**Partners**: Ammayi (Aunt) | **Date**: Jul 15, 2026

**Activity**: Data recovery, cloud storage migration, and device cleanup.

**Activity Log**

Ammayi was panicking because her smartphone storage was completely full, preventing her from taking new photos or receiving important files. Instead of buying expensive extra physical storage, I guided her through backing up her high-resolution photos to _Ente Photos_ (photo storage app, E2EE, cross-platform, open source and self-hostable). I also cleared out gigabytes of hidden app cache files and duplicate WhatsApp media that were clogging her system memory, restoring her phone's speed.

**Reflection**

Watching Ammayi's relief when her phone started running smoothly again showed me how critical basic data management skills are today. Most people treat a slow phone as a reason to buy a new one, but a little technical knowledge can solve the problem for free. Using my understanding of cloud systems to help family members maintain their digital tools is a practical way I can contribute.
