Day #6

**Partners**: Vallimma (Grandmother) | **Date**: Jul 12, 2026

**Activity**: Assisting with mobile device

**Activity Log**

Vallimma needed help swapping the SIM card in her classic Nokia keypad phone to a new network provider. She usually has one of my uncles do it, but since they were out of town, she turned to me for help. I popped open the back cover, removed the battery, replaced her old SIM card using a plastic adapter and reassembled it. We then made a quick test call to verify everything worked.

**Reflection**

Holding that sturdy little Nokia made me realize how much hardware has changed over time. Grandma can't handle smartphones, relying instead on that simple phone for her daily needs, and helping her fix it brought her so much peace of mind.
