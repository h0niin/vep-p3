Day #7

**Partners**: Moothemma (Aunt) | **Date**: Jul 13, 2026

**Activity**: Assisting with digital tools and navigating government portals.

**Activity Log**

Moothemma needed help submitting an online application on a local government portal for a property-related service, but she was completely stuck. The site was horribly built, filled with broken links, confusing drop-down menus, annoying captcha verifications and sudden session timeouts that kept wiping her progress. I stepped in to help compress her scanned files, and guided her step-by-step through the payment gateway until we finally generated the submission receipt.

**Reflection**

Trying to navigate that clunky website was immensely frustrating, and it made me realize how difficult the digital shift can be for older generations. When essential services move online without user-friendly design, it creates a real barrier for people who aren't tech-savvy. Helping Moothemma overcome a broken user interface saved her an exhausting trip to stand in line at a government office, showing me that digital literacy is as much about patience and troubleshooting as it is about knowing how to use a computer.
